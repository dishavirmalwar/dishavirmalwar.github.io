I am Disha Virmalwar, an Integrated BS-MS graduate in Physics from the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur. I am currently pursuing research in atmospheric science at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay under the supervision of Prof. Akshaya Nikumbh.

My research interests include Rossby wave breaking, atmospheric blocking, and their role in extreme weather and climate events. I am interested in understanding the large-scale atmospheric processes that contribute to persistent weather patterns and extremes.

Looking ahead, I hope to deepen my understanding of atmospheric dynamics and contribute to research that improves our understanding of extreme events and climate variability.
